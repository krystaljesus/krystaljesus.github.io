Isidora Seyrès – Recipient Rusts from Precipitation

1. Isidora Seyrès – Recipient Rusts from Precipitation

Interesting single that I very much enjoyed the process of making, which was mostly improvised. This single is kind of a representation of my evolution in terms of music gear, as this is composed of programmed cuts of four different improvisation sessions with modified household items as gear. The combination of all these makes for a fast moving, almost nauseous collage of analog and electronic sounds. I originally planned on producing a mini-CD of this single, which ended up making me improve on my visual collage techniques, which is now an art form I am very interested in.

Turntables, toys, tapes and various objects all by me. Makes use of the Max device "shhh" by nnirror.

Originally released on A Ten Pound Note Found In The Drain on the 1st of April 2026

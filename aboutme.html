About Me:

Hello, welcome to my website ! My name is Lorelei Lorelei, I’m an artist who works around the realm of sound collage and experimental art in general. I use field recordings, Ableton Live, tape machines, turntables, radios and various random items to produce my music. I am very open to the idea of collaboration, and am willing to collaborate with everyone. If you want to collaborate with me, you can send me an email at my address: lorelei130909@gmail.com
